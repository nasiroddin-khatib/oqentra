Good to know

Questions, answered.

Not sure where to start? These are a few things customers commonly want to understand.

Do I need any technical knowledge before contacting OQENTRA?+

No. You don't need any technical knowledge or prior experience to work with us. Simply explain your business idea, requirements, or what you want your application to achieve. Our team will take care of everything — understanding your requirements, planning, designing, developing, maintaining, managing, monitoring, troubleshooting, and updating your application.

You focus on your business. We'll take care of the technology.

Is application development included in the maintenance price?+

Application development and maintenance are two separate services with separate pricing.

  • Application Development: You pay a one-time development fee based on your application's features and complexity. This covers building your application.
  • Ongoing Maintenance Plan: After development, you can choose one of our maintenance plans for continuous application maintenance and management.

You can manage your application yourself if you have the technical resources, or choose an OQENTRA maintenance plan. With our plan, we take care of the ongoing technical responsibilities, so you don't have to worry about the day-to-day management of your application.

Are infrastructure charges included in the management plan?+

No separate infrastructure or monitoring charges are required from you. You only pay the agreed maintenance plan fee.

Our maintenance service covers the ongoing technical responsibilities, including infrastructure management, application monitoring, maintenance, troubleshooting, and updates, as agreed in your selected plan.

Application development is charged separately as a one-time fee.

Can OQENTRA work with an application we already have?+

Yes. We can work with applications that have already been developed, whether by another company, an independent developer, or your own team.

We'll first understand your existing application and its technical setup, then help you with its ongoing maintenance, management, monitoring, troubleshooting, improvements, and updates.

You don't need to rebuild your application or start from scratch to work with us.

What happens after I submit an enquiry?+

We'll review the details and contact you using the information you provide. A quotation or project commitment is made only after requirements and scope are discussed.

Do I have to create an account to browse or request a quote?+

No. You can explore the public website and send an enquiry without signing up.
