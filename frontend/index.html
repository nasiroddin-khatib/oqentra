CLOUD BILLING OPTIONS

Choose who pays the cloud provider.

Both options include infrastructure planning and management within the agreed scope. The difference is who owns the cloud account and receives the provider's bill.

OPTION 01

Your AWS account

You own the AWS account and pay AWS directly. We manage the agreed infrastructure using authorized access, such as a cross-account IAM role.

You payAWS charges directly + OQENTRA development and management fees.
OPTION 02

OQENTRA-managed hosting

We host the application in our AWS account and provide an agreed usage-based infrastructure charge with billing visibility and limits.

You payOQENTRA development and management fees + agreed infrastructure charges.

Cloud usage varies with traffic, storage, architecture and provider pricing. We provide an estimate, define usage controls and reconcile charges transparently. No unlimited hosting is implied.

07 / Good to know

Questions, answered.

Not sure where to start? These are a few things customers commonly want to understand.

Do I need to know the technology before contacting you?+

No. Explain what you want your business or customers to be able to do. We'll help translate that into a practical technical plan.

Is application development included in the maintenance price?+

No. Development is quoted separately based on features and complexity. Maintenance covers only the agreed ongoing services.

Are AWS charges included in the management plan?+

No. Cloud charges are estimated separately. You can pay AWS directly from your own account or choose an OQENTRA-managed hosting arrangement with transparent usage-based billing.

Can you work with an application we already have?+

Yes, subject to a technical review. We'll assess the current code, hosting, database, security and operational condition before recommending changes.

What happens after I submit an enquiry?+

We'll review the details and contact you using the information you provide. A quotation or project commitment is made only after requirements and scope are discussed.

Do I have to create an account to browse or request a quote?+

No. You can explore the public website and send an enquiry without signing up.
